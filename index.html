Welcome

Handmade bread and pastry, baked for the neighborhood since sunrise.

North Star Bakery is a small, community-centered bakery. Every loaf is mixed, shaped, and baked in-house, and every order — from a single croissant to a wedding cake — is treated like it matters, because it does.

What we're known for

Three things you can always count on

Naturally leavened bread

Our sourdough starter is over ten years old. Loaves are baked in small batches daily, never held over for the next morning.

Made-from-scratch pastry

Laminated dough is rolled by hand and croissants are shaped fresh each morning — no par-baked or frozen shortcuts.

Custom cakes for real life

Birthdays, showers, and neighborhood milestones. We work with you on flavor, size, and timeline for events big and small.
