Our story

Started with one oven and a neighborhood that showed up

North Star Bakery opened as a single countertop operation, selling loaves out of a home kitchen to neighbors who asked for more. Today it's a full storefront, but the goal hasn't changed: bake honest food for the people around us.

From kitchen table to Maple Street

We started baking sourdough for friends during a slow winter, and word spread faster than we expected. Within a year, weekend orders had outgrown a home kitchen, and neighbors on Maple Street helped us find our first real space.

We're still on that same block. Regulars know us by name, and a lot of our recipes started as someone's request.

Sourcing philosophy

Simple ingredients, sourced close to home

Local flour mill

Our bread flour comes from a family-run mill about two hours away, milled in small batches so it stays fresh.

Seasonal fruit

Galettes and tarts follow what's actually in season at the farmers market, not a fixed year-round menu.

No shortcuts

No par-baked bread, no premixed batters. If it's on the menu, it was mixed and shaped here.
